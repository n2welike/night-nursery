Privacy Policy

Night Nursery: Moon Garden (“the game”) for iPhone, iPad and Android. Effective 24 November 2026.

The short version

You don’t need an account or an internet connection to play. Your progress stays on your device. When the device is online, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics so we can see where it is too hard. There are no ads, no purchases and no tracking across other apps.

What stays on your device

Your nights, coins, decorations, settings and chosen language are saved locally on your device. We never receive this save. Choosing “Start fresh” in Settings replaces it with a new game; deleting the app removes it.

Anonymous statistics

The game sends short gameplay signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, for example: the game was opened, a night started or ended (night number, grade, stars), a daily gift was claimed, an item was bought in the in-game shop with in-game coins. Each signal carries a one-way SHA-256 hash of a random number the game creates on install, so signals from one install can be counted together without identifying you.

The statistics do not include your name, email, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifier or your save. We use them only in aggregate to improve the game. We do not sell them, share them with advertisers or link them to other data. If the device is offline, nothing is sent.

What the game does not do

  • No sign-in, no accounts, no cloud saves.
  • No ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No tracking across apps or websites (no App Tracking Transparency prompt is needed).
  • No access to camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can play fully offline. To clear the anonymous install number, delete and reinstall the game: a new random number is created. Questions or requests: see Support.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, for example by adding purchases, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
