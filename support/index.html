Common questions

Do I need the internet? No. The whole game works offline.

How do I change the language? Settings → Language. “Device language” follows your phone.

How do I start over? Settings → Start fresh. This erases your coins, upgrades and completed nights on this device.

Will my progress move to a new phone? Not yet: progress is saved only on the device where you play.

Are there ads or purchases? No.
